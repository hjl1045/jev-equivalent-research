# Complete synthetic boundary examples

Author-assigned labels were frozen before inference. These are short purpose-discrimination tests, not realistic long claims packets. All content is fictional.

## boundary_01 — demand_letter

Neighboring category: settlement_release

Counsel writes: Our client will accept $19,500 to resolve the collision injury claim. Enclosed is proposed release wording for discussion: claimant releases all bodily injury claims upon receipt of agreed funds. That wording is a draft enclosure, not an executed agreement. Your insured disputes the treatment duration. Please respond to this offer within fourteen days; we can discuss revisions to the proposed terms. No payment has been received and our client has signed nothing. The immediate action sought is your settlement response.

## boundary_05 — witness_statement

Neighboring category: police_report

Recorded interview of Avery Lane by officer Bell. Bell: Tell me what you saw. Lane: I was waiting at the bus stop when the delivery van struck the parked car. I heard the impact and saw the driver step out. Bell: Did you see a traffic signal? Lane: No; I could not see the intersection. Bell: Did either driver speak to you? Lane: The van driver asked if I was injured. I was not. The foregoing is my account, read back and approved by me, Avery Lane. The officer certified the recording, without adding investigative findings.

## boundary_06 — police_report

Based on my scene observations and the recorded statements, I documented an improper backing maneuver as the contributing action. Witness remarks above are quoted evidence within my investigation. Filed and signed by officer Bell.
